THE ART DIRECTION

A cozy town wins.

This is an original little world, inspired by the feeling of an old MMO hometown.

Ragnarok · the foundation
A town organized around memorable landmarks makes a natural portfolio map. Warm materials and quiet colors keep it welcoming.
MapleStory · the personality
Small characters, friendly copy, and a sense of discovery. A side-scroller would be delightful, but it adds travel time to finding your work.
World of Warcraft · a small influence
A quest log makes exploration tangible. Heavy armor frames and a dense action bar would compete with the portfolio itself.

The choice: an illustrated village, optional customization, and one-click entry. A journal keeps every section easy to reach.
